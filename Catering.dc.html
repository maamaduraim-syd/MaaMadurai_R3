How Catering Works

1

Tell us the headcount

Call, or send the enquiry form below with your date, guest numbers and whether you need vegetarian options.

2

We size the order

We tell you how many buckets, starters and sides your headcount needs — half and full buckets are our two sizes.

3

Confirm a day or two ahead

One to two days of notice is usually enough. For big weekend functions, a week is safer.

4

Collect hot from the shop

Pick up at Portico Plaza at your slot, packed hot and ready to serve. Delivery for larger functions on request.
