Travels Well

Some dishes are better in the box than others. If you are ordering delivery, these hold their texture best:

  • Madurai chicken and Seeraga Samba mutton biryani
  • Kothu parotta — it is meant to steam a little
  • Pepper chicken, mutton chukka and kola urundai
  • Goli soda and rose milk, straight from the fridge

Dosai and idly are best eaten fresh off the griddle — worth the walk in if you can.
