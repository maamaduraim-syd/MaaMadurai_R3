Getting Here

By car

Enter from Aurelia Street. Park free inside the Portico Plaza car park, or on the street out the front. Five minutes from Pendle Hill and Girraween, about ten from Wentworthville, Seven Hills and Winston Hills.

By train

Toongabbie station is a short walk away on the T1 Western Line — straight down Aurelia Street to Portico Plaza.

Landmark

We are inside the plaza, opposite Woolworths. Look for the red MaaMadurai shopfront at Shop P34.
