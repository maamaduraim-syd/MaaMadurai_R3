Drink guide

Jigarthanda is a cold Madurai drink layered from sweetened chilled milk, nannari (sarsaparilla) syrup, almond gum or kadal paasi jelly, and ice cream. The name means roughly “cool heart”.

Jigarthanda: the cold drink Madurai is famous for

The name translates roughly as "cool heart", and in a Madurai summer that is exactly how it is sold — from handcarts, in tall steel tumblers, to anyone walking past. Jigarthanda is built in layers: chilled milk sweetened and thickened, nannari (sarsaparilla) syrup for its herbal edge, soaked kadal paasi — a seaweed-based jelly — or almond gum for body, and a scoop of ice cream on top.

It is not a milkshake and it is not a falooda. The texture is the point: you should get chewy pieces, cold milk and melting ice cream in the same sip. Stir it once before you drink, then leave it alone.

Order it after something spicy — pepper chicken, mutton chukka or a Mysore masala dosai. If you want the other Madurai street classic, ask for a goli soda: the old marble-stoppered bottle you push open with your thumb.
